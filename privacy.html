Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

ArtLibrary (artlibrary.ai) is a library of AI artworks and the prompts behind them. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site and create an account, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you register with email, we store your name, username, email address, and a securely hashed password. When you sign in with Google, we receive from Google your name, email address, and basic profile, and we store your Google account ID to link the account.
  • Sign-in security data. We keep session records (a hashed session token and its expiry) and a small log of failed sign-in attempts to protect accounts from abuse.
  • Approximate sign-in details. When you create an account or successfully sign in, we store the approximate city and country provided by Cloudflare and a broad device type inferred from your browser's user-agent. The site owner can see signup and most recent sign-in details in the private admin dashboard; we do not store precise coordinates or the full user-agent string.
  • Cookies. We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show your username on your future public portfolio page (artlibrary.ai/your-username).
  • To contact you about the service when needed.

What we never do

  • We never store your password in plain text, and we never see your Google password.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from your account page. To change or delete your account, contact us and we will help.
