About ArtLibrary

ArtLibrary is a small library of AI-generated images, each shown together with the prompt used to make it.

The idea is simple: you browse the images, find one you like, and copy its prompt. You can also change the highlighted parts of a prompt, for example a name, a place or a color, to build your own version before you copy it.

What you can do today

  • Browse the gallery by category and AI model, and search by keyword.
  • Open any image to read its full prompt.
  • Copy the original prompt in one click.
  • Edit the highlighted parts of a prompt, copy your version, or reset it to the original.
  • Switch between dark and light themes.

What to know

  • ArtLibrary does not generate images. You paste the prompt into the AI image tool you prefer.
  • Results depend on the tool and model you use. A customized prompt can give a different result from the image shown.
  • The site is new and growing. Accounts, uploads, a community and email updates are not available yet.
